# Employee Handbook (Version 3.0)

## 1. Welcome
This handbook summarises the policies that apply to all employees. If this handbook conflicts with a specific policy (such as the Leave and Holiday Policy), the specific policy prevails.

## 2. Probation
New employees serve a probation period of 90 days from their joining date. During probation, earned leave and work from home are not available.

## 3. Notice Period
After confirmation, the notice period is 60 days for all employees. During probation, the notice period is 15 days. Leave cannot normally be taken during the notice period except sick leave.

## 4. Code of Conduct
Employees are expected to act with integrity, treat colleagues with respect, and protect confidential company and employee information. Harassment and discrimination of any kind are not tolerated.

## 5. Data Privacy
Employee personal data, including leave and attendance records, may be accessed only by the employee, their management chain, and HR for legitimate business purposes.

## 6. Grievances
Concerns may be raised with the reporting manager, the skip-level manager, or HR. Grievances are acknowledged within 2 working days.

## 7. Contacts
For questions about this handbook, contact the HR team through the HR helpdesk.
