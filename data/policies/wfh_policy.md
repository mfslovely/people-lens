# Work From Home Policy (Version 2.1)

## 1. Eligibility
All full-time employees who have completed the 90-day probation period are eligible to work from home (WFH). Employees on probation work from the office unless their manager grants an exception in writing.

## 2. Allowance
Eligible employees may work from home for up to 2 days per week. Unused WFH days do not carry over to the following week.

## 3. Approval
WFH days must be agreed with the reporting manager at least 1 working day in advance. Managers may limit WFH when team coverage in the office would fall below operational needs.

## 4. Expectations
Employees working from home must be reachable during core hours (10:00 to 16:00), attend scheduled meetings with video enabled where required, and record their attendance as "WFH" in the attendance system.

## 5. Equipment and Security
Employees must use company-issued laptops connected through the corporate VPN. Confidential documents must not be printed at home.
