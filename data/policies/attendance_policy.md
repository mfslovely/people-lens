# Attendance Policy (Version 1.0)

## 1. Working Hours
The standard working day is 9 hours including a 1-hour lunch break. Core hours are 10:00 to 16:00, when all employees are expected to be available.

## 2. Recording Attendance
Employees must record attendance every working day as Present, WFH, or On Leave. Attendance is reconciled with approved leave every week.

## 3. Late Arrival
Arrival after 10:15 is recorded as late. Three late arrivals in a calendar month result in a half-day casual leave deduction.

## 4. Unauthorised Absence
An absence without an approved leave request or prior intimation is recorded as Absent. Three or more unauthorised absences in a calendar month will be reviewed by HR and may lead to disciplinary action. Absence for 5 or more consecutive working days without contact is treated as job abandonment.

## 5. Inconsistent Records
Attendance marked "On Leave" without a matching approved leave request, or attendance marked "Present" during approved leave, is treated as an inconsistency and is corrected by HR during the monthly reconciliation.
