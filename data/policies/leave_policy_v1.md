# Leave and Holiday Policy (Version 1.0)

## 1. Scope
This policy applies to all full-time employees of the company. It is superseded by Version 2.0 effective 1 April 2026.

## 2. Casual Leave (CL)
Every employee is entitled to 12 days of casual leave per calendar year. Casual leave may be taken for a maximum of 3 consecutive working days. Casual leave cannot be carried forward and lapses on 31 December.

## 3. Sick Leave (SL)
Every employee is entitled to 10 days of sick leave per calendar year. Sick leave cannot be carried forward.

## 4. Earned Leave (EL)
Employees accrue 18 days of earned leave per calendar year. Up to 10 days of unused earned leave may be carried forward to the next calendar year. Earned leave requires at least 7 calendar days of advance notice.

## 5. Public Holidays
Public holidays are published annually in the holiday calendar.
