# Leave and Holiday Policy (Version 2.0)

## 1. Scope and Definitions
This policy applies to all full-time employees. It replaces Version 1.0 from 1 April 2026. A "working day" is any Monday to Friday that is not a public holiday. Weekends and public holidays that fall inside a leave period are not counted against any leave balance. The leave year is the calendar year (1 January to 31 December).

## 2. Casual Leave (CL)
### 2.1 Entitlement
Every employee is entitled to 12 days of casual leave per calendar year, credited at 1 day per month.
### 2.2 Usage rules
Casual leave may be taken for a maximum of 3 consecutive working days per request. Casual leave must be applied for at least 1 working day in advance, except in emergencies, which must be reported to the manager on the same day. Half-day casual leave is permitted.
### 2.3 Carry forward
Casual leave cannot be carried forward. Any unused casual leave lapses on 31 December.

## 3. Sick Leave (SL)
### 3.1 Entitlement
Every employee is entitled to 10 days of sick leave per calendar year.
### 3.2 Usage rules
Sick leave may be applied for retroactively within 7 calendar days of returning to work. A medical certificate is required for sick leave longer than 2 consecutive working days. Half-day sick leave is permitted.
### 3.3 Carry forward
Up to 5 days of unused sick leave may be carried forward to the next calendar year.

## 4. Earned Leave (EL)
### 4.1 Entitlement and accrual
Employees accrue 18 days of earned leave per calendar year, credited at 1.5 days per month.
### 4.2 Eligibility
Earned leave can be used only after completing the 90-day probation period.
### 4.3 Notice
Earned leave must be applied for at least 7 calendar days before the first day of leave. A single earned leave request may not exceed 15 consecutive working days.
### 4.4 Carry forward
Up to 15 days of unused earned leave may be carried forward to the next calendar year (increased from 10 days in Version 1.0). Earned leave above this limit lapses on 31 December.

## 5. Loss of Pay (LOP)
When an employee has exhausted the relevant leave balance, absence is treated as loss of pay. Loss-of-pay leave must be approved by HR in addition to the reporting manager.

## 6. Overlapping Requests
An employee may not hold two pending or approved leave requests that overlap on any date. A request that overlaps an existing one is rejected by the system.
